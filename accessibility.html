Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: 19 November 2026

I want this portfolio to be usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology.

This website is in English. It uses headings, text labels for contact fields, alternative text for key images, keyboard-operable links and form controls, a skip-to-content link, a main content landmark, and reduced-motion styling for visitors who request it in their device settings.

My accessibility target is Israeli Standard SI 5568 at level AA. The website has not undergone a full professional accessibility assessment, and I am not claiming full conformance. A preliminary review led to improvements to text contrast, keyboard focus, and page navigation, including a main content landmark and a skip link. These changes do not replace a full accessibility assessment. Screen-reader use, high zoom levels, all browsers, and the complete external form journey have not been fully assessed.

The contact form uses the external FormSubmit service. Its submission and anti-spam screens have not been fully assessed. If you cannot use the form, you can email me directly at contact@sagi-simhi.dev.

To report an accessibility problem or request this portfolio's information in another accessible format, contact Sagi Simhi at contact@sagi-simhi.dev. If possible, include the page or section, what you were trying to do, and the browser or assistive technology you use. You do not need to share medical information.
