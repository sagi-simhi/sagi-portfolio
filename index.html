Who I Am

About

I'm currently pairing a B.A. in Economics & Computer Science at the Open University of Israel with self-directed work in portfolio construction and behavioral fintech design - the kind of projects nobody assigns you, that you build because you want to see the answer for yourself.

That habit comes from outside finance. Years spent supervising operational shifts and leading teams under pressure - first as a tactical operator, later coordinating international operations and running a SOC floor - taught me to read a situation quickly, trust the data over the narrative, and build a process rather than react to the next fire. I bring the same instincts to reading a portfolio or a chart: structure first, then the story.

Right now that means building small, honest tools - a portfolio dashboard, a technical-analysis lab - rather than talking about finance in the abstract.
