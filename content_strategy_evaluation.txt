# Content Strategy Evaluation - sagi-simhi.dev Portfolio
## Date: 2026-08-15
## Evaluator: Claude Code Assistant
## Perspective: Recruiter/Hiring Manager for Data Analyst, BI Analyst, Product Analyst Roles

## Executive Summary
This evaluation analyzes all textual content in the portfolio from a recruiter's perspective. The goal is to assess whether the content effectively communicates analytical thinking, data literacy, technical execution, product mindset, attention to detail, and professional communication - the key traits sought in data analyst candidates.

## Overall Assessment
The portfolio shows strong foundational content with clear communication of technical skills and operational experience. However, several sections could be strengthened to better demonstrate analytical capabilities and data-driven thinking specifically relevant to data analyst roles.

## Main Website Content Analysis

### Hero Section (lines 59-89)
**Current Content:**
- Eyebrow: "Economics × Computer Science"
- Title: "Economics & CS Student Building Data-Driven Financial Tools"
- Bio: "Combining operational discipline under pressure with Economics and Computer Science studies to build practical financial tools."
- CTA: "View My Projects" and GitHub link

**What it's trying to communicate:**
Interdisciplinary background (Economics + CS), practical application focus, operational discipline translated to analytical work.

**Effectiveness for recruiters:**
✅ Strong - Immediately establishes the unique value proposition of Economics + CS combination
✅ Good - Focus on "building practical financial tools" shows execution orientation
⚠️ Could be stronger - "Financial tools" slightly misaligned with pure data analyst roles (may imply finance specialization over general analytics)

**Demonstrates analytical ability:**
Partially - Shows applied skills but could emphasize analysis/interpretation more than building

**Recommendation:** 
Refine to emphasize data analysis and interpretation: "Economics & CS Student Translating Data into Business Insights" or similar.

### Projects Section (lines 155-178)
**Current Content:**
Two project cards with brief descriptions:
1. "Factor-Tilted Portfolio Dashboard - Tracks an 80/20 FTSE All-World / Small-Cap Value portfolio against the S&P 500 and MSCI ACWI."
2. "Financial Intelligence Platform - An AI-powered investment intelligence product concept - in active development."

**What it's trying to communicate:**
Technical project experience, financial domain knowledge, initiative in building complex tools.

**Effectiveness for recruiters:**
⚠️ Mixed - Project descriptions are too brief and finance-focused
❌ Weak - Doesn't highlight analytical aspects, methodologies, or insights gained
⚠️ Concerning - "Investment intelligence" may imply focus on trading/investing over general analytics
❌ Missed opportunity - Doesn't show what was learned or how it demonstrates analytical skills

**Demonstrates analytical ability:**
❌ Poor - Focuses on what was built, not what was analyzed or learned

**Recommendation:**
Transform project descriptions to highlight analytical process:
- Instead of "Tracks a portfolio..." → "Analyzed portfolio construction methodologies to develop an 80/20 global equity/small-cap value strategy, evaluating factor tilt impact on risk-return profiles through simulated backtesting"
- Instead of "AI-powered investment intelligence..." → "Designed and prototyped an AI-augmented financial analysis system, conducting user research to identify key insights for investment decision-making processes"
