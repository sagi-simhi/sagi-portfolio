Product case study · In development

Turning scattered investment information into a structured decision workspace

This concept explores how portfolio data, research notes, risk context, and AI explanations could live in one transparent interface without presenting automated trading advice.

Concept, not a live product
01 · Problem

Information is fragmented

Portfolio positions, market context, research notes, and learning resources often sit in separate tools, making comparison and reflection harder.

02 · Approach

Design around decisions

Organize the experience around questions: what is owned, why it is owned, what changed, and which assumptions require review.

03 · System

Modular workspace

Separate portfolio monitoring, structured research, educational context, and AI assistance into focused but connected views.

04 · Guardrails

Explain, do not prescribe

AI is framed as a research explainer. Sources, assumptions, uncertainty, and product limitations should remain visible.

Proposed workflow

From signal to review

  1. 01

    Observe

    Surface portfolio changes and relevant context without implying a recommendation.

  2. 02

    Investigate

    Connect an observation to structured company, ETF, or portfolio research.

  3. 03

    Explain

    Use plain-language assistance to clarify concepts, assumptions, and trade-offs.

  4. 04

    Reflect

    Record the reasoning behind a decision and revisit it as evidence changes.
