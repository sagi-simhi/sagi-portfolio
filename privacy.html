Privacy Notice

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Who operates this website

This personal portfolio is operated by Sagi Simhi. For privacy questions, contact contact@sagi-simhi.dev.

Information you choose to provide

If you use the contact form, I receive the name, email address and message you submit. Providing information is voluntary, not a legal requirement. The form currently requires these fields so I can receive and respond to your enquiry. You may instead contact me by email. Please do not include passwords, identity documents, financial account details, medical information or other sensitive information.

How information is used

I use your information to respond to your enquiry and manage related correspondence. I do not use contact-form submissions to subscribe people to marketing. I do not sell contact-form information.

Services that process information

The form sends submissions through FormSubmit to contact@sagi-simhi.dev and Cloudflare Email Routing and Gmail for receipt and storage, and Zoho Mail for replies sent from my domain. FormSubmit's help page states that submissions are retained by the service for 30 days. That does not determine how long a copy remains in my email account. FormSubmit and email providers may process information outside Israel. Their processing is also subject to their own terms and privacy notices. The countries of processing and all FormSubmit subcontractors have not been fully verified.

This website also uses Cloudflare services for delivery, security and website-performance analytics. A Cloudflare analytics beacon loads when the page is viewed. Cloudflare states that its Web Analytics measurements do not use cookies or localStorage to measure visitors or fingerprint individuals. Hosting and security services may still handle technical connection and request data. External form or anti-spam screens may use additional technologies under their providers' policies.

Retention

My retention policy is to review routine contact enquiries periodically and delete those no longer needed, normally within 12 months after our last substantive exchange, unless an ongoing opportunity, a legal obligation or the protection of legal rights requires longer retention. This is a manual review policy, not an automatic deletion feature. Provider copies and backups may follow their own retention schedules.

Your choices and rights

You may contact me to request access to your personal information or ask for inaccurate, incomplete, unclear or outdated information to be corrected, in accordance with applicable Israeli law. You may also ask me to delete correspondence; I will consider the request subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate retention needs. I may need enough information to verify that a request concerns your own information.

External links

Links to LinkedIn and GitHub take you to services governed by their own privacy notices.

Changes and contact

I may update this notice if the website or its services change. The update date will appear above. Contact: Sagi Simhi, contact@sagi-simhi.dev.
